AI Customer Support

Drafts replies from your own policies and FAQs, and escalates what it can't answer.

Role: Customer support · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the customer message or ticket export, and policies and FAQs in Shared/Policies and Shared/FAQ in Shared/Support, then ask in plain words for reply drafts; an escalation list and a common-questions log. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Customer Support covers

customer service · support tickets · complaint handling · FAQ · helpdesk · ticket triage

What the AI Customer Support needs from you

Needs

  • The customer message or ticket export.
  • Policies and FAQs in Shared/Policies and Shared/FAQ.

Helpful

  • Order or invoice details.
  • Helpdesk access.

How the AI Customer Support works

  1. Identify the real question, urgency and sentiment of each message.
  2. Find the answer in your policies and FAQs; quote the policy line used.
  3. Draft a short, polite reply in the customer's language.
  4. Escalate anything not covered (refund exceptions, legal threats, safety) with a summary for the owner, and log recurring questions for the FAQ.

What you get from the AI Customer Support

  • Reply drafts.
  • An escalation list.
  • A common-questions log.

Everything is saved in Shared/Support on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every reply cites a policy or FAQ entry.
  • No invented prices, dates or promises.
  • Tone is polite and in the customer's language.

Done when: each message has a grounded draft or an escalation with reason.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending replies.
  • Issuing refunds or replacements.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • Helpdesk / inbox (Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, Gmail): in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add the AI Customer Support

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Customer Support” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Customer Support free?

Yes. The AI Customer Support comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Customer Support need from me?

The customer message or ticket export and policies and FAQs in Shared/Policies and Shared/FAQ. It also helps to have order or invoice details and helpdesk access.

Does the AI Customer Support act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending replies and issuing refunds or replacements. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Customer Support keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Support. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
